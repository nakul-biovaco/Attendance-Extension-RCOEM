SUPPORT, DOCUMENTATION & PRIVACY

Support

Need help or facing synchronization issues? Make sure you are logged in to your student portal and refresh the attendance page.

  • Auto-Sync: Opens student attendance table and reads live records.
  • Reset: Use the reset button above if attendance counts get stuck.
  • Contact: Built by students for students of RCOEM / RBU.

Documentation

Operation 75 helps you manage your semester attendance simply and accurately:

  • Target Attendance: Default 75% overall and 60% per-course minimum.
  • Safe Bunk: Calculates how many classes you can skip without falling below target.
  • Classes to Maintain: Shows exact classes needed to reach 75%.
  • Term Forecast: Projects your final percentage at semester end.

Privacy Policy

Your data belongs entirely to you. We follow strict privacy-first principles:

  • 100% Local: All data is stored directly in your browser's local storage.
  • Zero External Servers: No personal details, passwords, or attendance data are ever transmitted outside.
  • Full Control: You can export or delete all stored data with a single click.
